# Systems inspiration matrix (not reverse-engineered source evidence)

| Reference | Design principle to study | Original SABLESTAR subsystem | What we must independently test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kingdom Hearts IV | Fast melee/magic flow; published gameplay footage only | Resonance stance chaining | Input latency, cancel windows and camera control |
| Donkey Kong Bananza | Readable destructive traversal | Crystals now; destructible voxel/material world later | Collision rebuild budgets and deterministic fragments |
| Final Fantasy XV | Companion interactions and camping/vehicle pacing | Warden orb; traveling cohort later | Ally navigation and utility without stealing control |
| Baldur's Gate 3 | Consequence-tracked choices and combinatorial encounters | Dual faction gate with explicit flags | Durable, replay-safe choices and branching dialogue |
| Elden Ring | Commitment-driven attack timing and enemy tell | Windup/stagger stamina combat | Hitboxes, i-frames, poise fairness and camera |
| Skyrim | High reactivity and place discovery | Four planned regions, systemic faction affinity | Quest idempotence and evolving world state |
| BOTW / TOTK | Generalizable environmental objects and physics interactions | Modular interactable and breakable objects | Systemic object combinatorics and performance |
| Resident Evil Requiem | Resource pressure, threat pacing and limited visibility | Underground survival-tension design | Audio and lighting cues, scarcity and accessibility |

No mechanics above imply access to original source or permission to copy third-party commercial assets.
