# Art direction — Golden Hour in a Living Valley

Mood: **gentle wonder, hand-crafted rural charm, visible seasonal life**. Do not reproduce any commercial Harvest Moon texture, portrait, character likeness, map or music. Rebuild with independently authored colors, shapes, sounds and mechanics.

- Silhouette: rounded timber homes, clustered wildflowers, low timber fences, stepped foothills and orchards; environmental landmarks immediately recognizable.
- Light: peach dawn, warm honey afternoons, soft lavender twilight, visible soft shadows and a bright moving day cycle.
- Seasonal palette: Spring mint/blossom; Summer emerald/golden sun; Autumn amber/crimson leaves; Winter cobalt/powder snow.
- Camera: comfortable elevated third-person orbit with adjustable distance and non-disorienting movement.
- Sound: original gentle instrumental motifs, wind and distant birds, rain on roofs, seasonal insects, individualized villager/animal reactions; all independently composed and licensed.
- Interaction: tactile crop stages, soil moisture/gloss change, petting animations, fishing line behavior, harvest confetti, weather-aware clothing and footstep effects.
- Accessibility: non-color-only maturity/weather cues, scalable captions/UI, reduced motion/flash options, remappable keyboard/controller/touch inputs.
- Performance: instancing, LOD/impostors, light budgets, deterministic weather and serialized world chunks; profile actual devices.
- Asset sources: `assets/provenance.json`. Original authored elements recorded as such; external materials require license/source/hash and redistribution approval.

Current build uses simple *original primitive geometry only*; illustrated art, original audio and author-quality environments remain work in progress.
