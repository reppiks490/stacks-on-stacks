## Player experience
A visible **Ask a neighbor [U]** button appears beneath the work control. Walk within 4.2 world units of a resident, then tap/click or press U (controller right-stick click). The valley pauses; choose from two original resident-specific questions and up to two additional earned chapters (a completed shift and 80 friendship) using pointer/touch, keyboard 1/2, arrow keys and Enter, or D-pad and A. Escape/B cancels. While the menu is open, a 44-pixel **English/Español** button, keyboard **C**, or controller **X** switches both the topic labels and the spoken-response language without closing the modal, reordering topic IDs, or awarding extra friendship. The selected resident replies through the existing HUD, and the first authentic conversation that day is retained in the existing **Neighbors & Memories** journal. There are 24 original English/Spanish topic replies across six residents, with bilingual labels and menu instructions; 12 earned chapters remain locked until their prerequisites are satisfied. Journal locale selection drives the topic UI; unsupported locales continue to use English. This does not claim complete game localization.

## Authoritative state and precedence
The overlay never writes saves, currency, items, energy, friendship or restoration directly. It calls `village.act("talk", hero.global_position, topic_id)` only after a second proximity check; Village retains the original 56-day/year monotonic `day_epoch`, once-per-day talk reward, social contacts, festival logic and capped 12-entry transcript. A topic does not create a new persistent field. The first spoken line per resident per epoch is authoritative; repeated same-day questions can be heard but cannot overwrite that record or repeat rewards. Festival, rain and pending gift recollections take precedence over optional topics; invalid IDs fall back to established contextual dialogue. No additional social reputation, romance, quest or restoration rewards are invented.

## V19 keyboard/gamepad focused-control activation — bounded increment (2026-11-02)

- **Defect:** The topic overlay's global Enter/controller-A handler always chose the last D-pad-selected topic, even when keyboard Tab/Shift-Tab or pointer focus was on **English/Español**, **Not now**, or a different conversation button. Confirming a focused language/cancel control could unintentionally start a social interaction and award the first talk of the day.
- **Player action and feedback:** Enter and controller A now dispatch to the actual focused control: language toggles in place; cancel closes without dialogue; a focused topic plays its own authored response. D-pad Up/Down still selects topics; existing number shortcuts, controller X/B, mouse/touch presses, bilingual labels, pause restoration and nearest-resident reach checks remain. Empty topic lists cannot navigate a nonexistent button.
- **State semantics and regression:** No save schema or economy changes. Only an explicitly confirmed topic can invoke Village's authoritative `act("talk")`; locale/cancel confirmations cannot change affection, transcript, inventory, petals or restoration. The existing native assembled-game acceptance adds focused-language Enter/A, focused-cancel Enter/A, focus-selected alternate topic, modal/pause invariants and resource conservation. Source-level synthetic input is not physical keyboard/controller/touch verification.
- **Ownership/dependencies:** Only Village-owned topic panel, its native acceptance and this document change. Principal retains shared input/HUD/save, Farming and Construction/Emergence remain untouched; the 220-concept innovation implementation gate remains closed. Godot 4.x only.
- **Original assets and budget:** Built-in Godot focus widgets and existing authored bilingual text; no imported assets. Focus dispatch scans at most four topic controls on a confirm (O(4)), with no additional per-frame work or save bytes. Frame times, touch ergonomics, screen-reader focus and actual mobile viewport clipping remain unmeasured. **V19/V05 partial; exact-HEAD native CI and integration evidence must be recorded in issue #2.**
