# EVERBLOOM V05 — Original player-selectable NPC conversation topics

Status: **implementing; isolated candidate until exact-HEAD native CI and integration**. Mission IDs V05, V19, V03, V20. Godot 4.x only; no engine port. This document is an interface and acceptance contract, not a claim of a finished narrative system.

## Player experience
A visible **Ask a neighbor [U]** button appears beneath the work control. Walk within 4.2 world units of a resident, then tap/click or press U (controller right-stick click). The valley pauses; choose either of two authored, resident-specific questions using pointer/touch, keyboard 1/2, arrow keys and Enter, or D-pad and A. Escape/B cancels. The selected resident replies through the existing HUD, and the first authentic conversation that day is retained in the existing **Neighbors & Memories** journal. There are twelve original English/Spanish topic replies, two per resident, plus bilingual topic labels and menu instructions. Journal locale selection drives the topic UI; unsupported locales continue to use English. This does not claim complete game localization.

## Authoritative state and precedence
The overlay never writes saves, currency, items, energy, friendship or restoration directly. It calls `village.act("talk", hero.global_position, topic_id)` only after a second proximity check; Village retains the original 56-day/year monotonic `day_epoch`, once-per-day talk reward, social contacts, festival logic and capped 12-entry transcript. A topic does not create a new persistent field. The first spoken line per resident per epoch is authoritative; repeated same-day questions can be heard but cannot overwrite that record or repeat rewards. Festival, rain and pending gift recollections take precedence over optional topics; invalid IDs fall back to established contextual dialogue. No additional social reputation, romance, quest or restoration rewards are invented.

## Assets, device controls and performance
All words and UI primitives are original. No protected game models, music, voice, textures or external assets. Future portraits, gestures, voice and cinematics require original or independently rights-verified assets. The menu allocates two focusable buttons once per Village setup and checks two options per open; lookup and nearest-NPC selection are bounded by the current six residents. An O(1) locale check occurs during the panel's `_process`. **No measured frame-time, memory, screen-reader, mobile or physical-controller evidence** is claimed. The 44/56-pixel minimum controls are source-level touch targets, not proof of device ergonomics.
